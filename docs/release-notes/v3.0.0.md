## Airwave 3

Airwave is now a system-wide Audio Unit host for Mac. Build a separate effect chain for each audio output, and adjust the volume of individual apps in App Mixer or from the menu bar.

- **Effects** lets you add and reorder Audio Units, bypass them individually, and open their controls. Airwave includes Spatial and an Equalizer that reads EqualizerAPO presets.
- **App Mixer** lets you adjust or mute individual apps. Airwave remembers your adjustments.
- **Output profiles** keep each device’s effects and stereo channel assignment, including while the device is disconnected.

### Upgrading from Airwave 2

Airwave imports your saved output profiles and presets. An output gets a Spatial or Equalizer effect if it had that preset assigned in Airwave 2. When both were assigned, Spatial comes first.

macOS may ask Airwave for system-audio permission again. Allow access when prompted, or open **Settings → Setup & Troubleshooting** in Airwave and choose **Test Audio Access**.

Requires macOS 15 or later.
