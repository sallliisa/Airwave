## Airwave 3

Airwave has grown from a headphone spatializer into a system-wide Audio Unit host for macOS. The Spatial effect you know is now one part of an effect chain you can build for each output. You can also adjust the volume of individual apps.

### What’s new

- **An effect chain for each output.** Keep Spatial and EQ on your headphones, use a different chain on your speakers, or leave an output’s chain empty. Add compatible Audio Units under **Effects → Add Effect…**. Drag cards to reorder them, open their controls by clicking the card, and bypass individual effects without losing their settings. You can add the same Audio Unit more than once.
- **Spatial and Equalizer are included.** Spatial still supports compatible HeSuVi WAV presets for stereo headphones. Equalizer loads Equalizer APO presets and shows the stereo frequency response. Both are available under the **Airwave** publisher in the effect picker and in compatible AUv3 hosts after you open Airwave from Applications.
- **App Mixer.** Turn down or mute an individual app before its audio reaches the effect chain. Airwave remembers adjustments as you switch outputs. The menu bar gives you quick access to playing and recent apps, and the main window lets you manage saved adjustments.
- **The same macOS output controls.** Use macOS to switch speakers or headphones and adjust overall volume. The Airwave device picker selects the chain you’re editing, so you can configure a saved output while another is playing. Supported multichannel interfaces can still use a custom left/right channel pair.

### If you’re upgrading from Airwave 2

Update in place. Airwave imports your saved output profiles, channel assignments, compatible presets, and menu bar preference on first launch. Your original preset files stay on disk.

An output only gets a Spatial or Equalizer card if you had a preset assigned for that effect in Airwave 2. If both were assigned, the chain is **Spatial → Equalizer**. If an assigned preset can’t be loaded, Airwave leaves that effect bypassed until you choose a replacement in its controls.

**macOS may ask for system-audio permission again after the update.** Allow access when prompted. If audio is playing but effects or the mixer have no effect, go to **Settings → Setup & Troubleshooting → Test Audio Access**. The test plays a short sound and tells you whether capture is working.

Closing the window leaves Airwave processing in the background. Use the main power button to bypass both the effects and App Mixer, or choose **Quit Airwave** to stop processing and exit.

### Requirements and help

Airwave 3 requires **macOS 15 Sequoia or later** on Intel or Apple silicon and a supported physical audio output. Virtual and aggregate devices are unsupported as playback targets. Airwave uses System Audio Capture and does not request microphone access.

See the [Airwave guide](https://github.com/sallliisa/Airwave#readme) for setup, supported Equalizer APO syntax, and troubleshooting. If something goes wrong after updating, [open an issue](https://github.com/sallliisa/Airwave/issues) with your macOS version, output device, and the affected app or effect.
